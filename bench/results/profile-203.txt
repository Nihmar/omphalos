GEMVs by tensor size: >= 20 MiB at 89-94 % of 318 GB/s, 13-16 MiB at 74-80 %, ~2 MiB at 50-66 %.
Cause (bench/launch/stream_size.hip, a pure streaming read, back-to-back launches over a 2 GiB
pool): 12.9 MiB 50.2 us (GEMV 55.0), 21.5 MiB 77.3 (78.6), 36.5 MiB 127.1 (132.4), i.e. ~300 GB/s
plus 3-11 us per launch. The GEMVs are within 1-5 us of a kernel that only reads: their loss is
the per-launch fill / drain of the memory pipeline (~3.6 ms per step over ~420 GEMV launches)
plus 318 -> ~302 GB/s at these sizes; with the 2.5 ms of idle gaps, ~6 ms per step is the cost
of launching ~700 kernels. Lever: fewer, larger launches (the tensors that share an input in
one launch; .omph may store them concatenated), small kernels folded into neighbours.
