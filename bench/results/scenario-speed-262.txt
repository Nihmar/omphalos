## What each part is worth

| scenario | plain | drafter alone (mtp / dflash) | n-gram on top (mtp / dflash) |
|---|---:|---|---|
| prose (700-word story) | 24.3 | ×1.82 / ×1.81 | ×1.00 / ×1.00 |
| edit-cc (options.cc + a task) | 24.1 | ×3.25 / ×5.83 | ×3.24 / ×1.84 |
| edit-py (pelican.py + a task) | 24.0 | ×3.16 / ×3.87 | ×2.89 / ×2.64 |
| edit-py-think (same, thinking on) | 23.7 | ×2.35 / ×2.63 | ×1.13 / ×1.05 |

- The **n-gram** (#199) is worth nothing without repetition (`prose` ×1.00, it
  fires only when the 4..8-gram ending at the next token occurred before) and
  everything on edits: ×2.8-3.2 on the two edit scenarios once MTP is already
  drafting, i.e. 216-258 t/s from a 24 t/s baseline.
- The **drafter** is what the non-repetitive prompt gets: ×1.8 on prose, ×2.4-5.8
  on the edits. DFlash2 drafts more (15 masks against MTP's 3+1 chain: 1824 vs 1323
  drafts on prose) and accepts relatively less (30 % vs 41 %), yet it overtakes MTP
  on `edit-cc` (140.6 vs 78.4 without the n-gram) because its block drafts the
  copied spans in one pass.
- Thinking (`edit-py-think`) halves both: the copyable spans are a smaller part of
  a 12k-token answer, so the n-gram adds only ×1.05-1.13.
- **Under sampling** (temp 0.6, top-k 20, top-p 0.95, seed 262, MTP): the n-gram's
  gain holds - `edit-cc` 75.8 -> 212.9 t/s (×2.81) against greedy's ×3.24, `prose`
  unchanged (42.0 -> 41.5) - which is what the point-mass rule of #394 predicts
  (a draft is kept when the sample agrees with it).

## Identity at temperature 0

The same bytes out of all three configurations, `prose`, n-gram on (sha256 of the
completion, `7582511ba7cbd72d`): plain, MTP and DFlash2. The n-gram (or the
drafter) proposes, it never decides; the token-level witnesses are #122's
(verification + rollback) and #161's (bit-identical speculation).

## Reading

Both parts are worth keeping and neither is redundant: the drafter carries the
prompts with no repetition to exploit, the n-gram carries the edits and quotes
(the coding-agent case, where the context is quoted back), and the two compose
(x10.6 on `edit-cc`). No default changes follow from this record.
