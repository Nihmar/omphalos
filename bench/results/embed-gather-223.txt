Prompt embedding in one gather + one dequant (#223, P5 of #203), RX 9060 XT, 2026-10-03
Runner::embed dequantized the token embedding one row per launch (from the pinned host
table): 4096 launches for a 4k prompt, 32768 at 32k. Forwards of more than 4 tokens
now upload the ids (async), gather the rows into a device buffer (one kernel) and
dequantize them in one launch; decode steps and verifications keep a dequant per row.

Identity: logits (last 64 rows, wiki1000) and 8 decode tokens byte-identical.
