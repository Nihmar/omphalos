# M3.1 — fused Q4_K GEMV + load-time repacking (2026-09-30)

Tensor: `output.weight` (248320 x 5120, Q4_K, 682.0 MiB) — the largest single
tensor of the model, so the most bandwidth-hungry GEMV of the decode path.
Conditions: `omph-gemv-bench --iters 50` (warm-up 5), median of 5 runs,
ROCm 7.2.4, gfx1200, desktop idle (PLAN.md §17).
Ceiling: 318.3 GB/s measured (`bench/results/m0-bandwidth.csv`).

## Summary (median of 5 runs)

| path | time | weight bytes read | effective bandwidth | vs ceiling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| fused Q4_K GEMV (repacked) | **2.90 ms** | 701.0 MiB | **253.2 GB/s** | **79.6 %** |
| f16 path (dequant + hipBLASLt) | 82.97 ms | 2425.0 MiB | 30.6 GB/s | 9.6 % |

The fused kernel is **28.6x faster** than the milestone-2 path for this GEMV and
already clears the milestone-3 target (>= 60 % of measured bandwidth).

Repacking: 715,161,600 -> 735,027,200 bytes (+2.8 %, the 6-bit scales and mins are
decoded to bytes), verified by rebuilding the source block stream and comparing
**byte for byte** (`byte-identical rebuild: yes`).

Accuracy against the f16 path (same activations, f16 rounding only): max |d| 6e-4
(1.9e-4 relative), mean |d| 1e-4, |ref| up to 3.0.
