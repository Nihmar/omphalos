## The WY chunk size: worth 0.7 %, not worth the work

The delta-net prefill's serial part is `delta_wy_b_kernel`: one launch per (layer, prefill
chunk), 48 blocks (one per value head), each walking a 512-token chunk's eight 64-token WY
chunks in order (kwC = 64). 540 ms of 35.3 s at 32k, and its share *shrinks* with length
because it is linear in tokens while the attention is quadratic.

Doubling kwC to 128 would halve the sequential depth: -270 ms, **-0.8 % of the prefill**.
Against that: the intra-chunk work of phase A scales with C^2 (the WY inverse is C^3 per
chunk, T/C chunks: 2x at C = 128), the C x C f32 staging (`am[kWyC * kWyAld]`) goes from
17 KB to 67 KB and no longer fits the 64 KB LDS budget at all, and the WY form's own KL
(0.0002-0.0005 vs the exact path, #240) would have to be re-measured since a larger inverse
is the one thing that can move it. A kernel restructure for 0.8 % is not a trade this engine
should make. Closed with the number.

## What the table says about the rest

The prefill is a GEMM with an attention attached (75 % + 15 %), and both are at measured
structural walls, not at unexplored ones:

- **GEMM, 53-62 TFLOPS.** The practical WMMA ceiling is ~87 with random operands (~107 with
  constants), and the kernel's own ablations (bench/gemm/tile_ablate.hip, #213) put the LDS
  B-operand read at ~61 TFLOPS, the weight loads at 58, the WMMA-only body at 91. All three
  fixes have been tried and measured: the 4x4 shape (4x the B reuse) loses 25 % to VGPR
  pressure (`prefill-gemm-4x4-421.txt`), the int8 iu8 path sustains 42.8-46.1 TOPS against
  gemm_q's 53 because IQ3_S's per-32-weight scale forces a rescale every 2 WMMAs
  (`int8-gemm-213.txt`), and the B-operand LDS/vector split is a wash (`#457`). The register
  file holds the accumulator and the operands at once; that is the equilibrium.
- **Attention, 15 %.** The flash kernel is issue-bound at 240 VGPRs / 6 waves/SIMD (#191,
  #422); the small-query-count tail now takes the one-row-tile shape (#469, -4.4 % of the
  agent turn at 60k).
- **The small kernels, 5.7 %** (`add_rms_norm` + the delta-net's conv/gate + the attention's
  prep and merge): each is bandwidth-bound with few VALU ops to hide, and every one is
  already batched over 4-8 rows or heads (#240). Fusing the two norms into a neighbouring
  epilogue is the only idea left, and it is ~2 % at the risk of a norm reduction order
  change.
