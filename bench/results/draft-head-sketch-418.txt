# #418: a sketched draft head — the candidate recall kills it

Engine main 0571d06. Offline probe, no GPU: the head's first 98304 rows
(`output.weight`, Q4_K, dequantized with `gguf.quants.dequantize`), the probe
vectors = the target's layer-63 hidden states of a 400-token prompt
(`omph-run --trace-dir`, real hidden states, RMS-normalized with
`output_norm.weight` as the head's input is), 256 probes. The sketch is a
Johnson-Lindenstrauss projection: `S = H P`, `Xq = X P` with a fixed-seed
Gaussian `P` (5120 x rank), the sketch scores `Xq S^T` against the true `X H^T`.
Method implemented in `tools/sketch_probe.py`; the run takes ~5 minutes (the
projected vocabulary is the cost).

| rank | recall@16 | top-1 agreement | sketch, f16 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 128 | 0.0 % | 0.0 % | 24 MiB |
| 256 | **34.5 %** | 24.2 % | 48 MiB |
| 512 | **47.4 %** | 21.9 % | 96 MiB |

## Reading

The drafter feeds the selector the top-16 candidates of each block position and
their values; a sketch with a third of the true top-16 would change most
candidates and degrade the drafted tokens, and the selector's successor /
predecessor tables are built over the full vocabulary ids (the sketch only
reorders them).

The prize side, measured in #414: the drafter's head pass costs **2.9 ms per
step** (the full 682 MiB head against the 98304-row prefix). A rank-256 sketch
would cut that to ~0.2 ms, i.e. **~2.5-2.7 ms of a ~57 ms step (4.5 %)**, at the
price of an `.omph` format bump, a new tensor and a new GEMV. Against a 34.5 %
candidate recall that is not a trade worth proposing.

A rank-512 sketch (96 MiB) reaches 47.4 % and still costs less than the prefix
head it replaces, but the recall curve says the head's top-16 needs a much
higher rank than 256 (the vocabulary's rows are near-orthogonal in 5120 dims, so
the Johnson-Lindenstrauss bound for 98k competitors is not met at these ranks).
A truncated-SVD sketch would do better than a random projection at the same
rank, but the order of magnitude (a >50 % loss of candidates at rank 512) does
not suggest it changes the conclusion.

Decision: **no sketched draft head.** The candidates stay with the real head,
already reduced to the first 98304 rows (#217, #245).
