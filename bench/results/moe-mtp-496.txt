## Why K > 1 does not pay here

A verified token reads its own 8 experts: 3 tokens read ~2.2x the expert bytes
of one, and the experts are a large part of the step, so the verification's
rows are not cheap the way the dense model's are. Acceptance per draft is
44-56 % at K = 2 (77-85 % for the first draft).
