# n-gram (prompt lookup) drafts on the MoE model (#513)

Model: Ornith-1.5-35B-A3B-Q4_K_M (omphalos: the .omph with the calibrated expert
set, main a9f348a; llama.cpp: the pin v0.6.0 @ d8123504, build-hip-pin,
-ncmoe 16 - the file does not fit 16 GB). KV Q4/Q4 on both sides (omphalos
OMPH_KV_K4=1, llama.cpp -ctk q4_0 -ctv q4_0). tools/scenario_speed.py, greedy,
the prompt sent as the same token ids to both engines; one server per
(spec, n-gram) cell. omphalos' MTP drafts 1 token per step (its MoE default,
#496), llama.cpp's 3 (the tool's setting, its default). The plain n-gram=1
cells are a repeat of plain (the n-gram drafts need a drafter on both
engines): their spread is the run-to-run noise.

Every omphalos cell produced plain greedy's text (text_sha256 equal per
scenario). llama.cpp's greedy text differs from omphalos' (other numerics) and
stops much earlier on the edit scenarios (127-1126 tokens against 2083 / 4190),
so its rates are over shorter texts with fewer copies to look up.
