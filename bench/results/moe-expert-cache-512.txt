## Prefill and load

| | main | #512 |
|---|---:|---:|
| prefill 4096 (wiki16k, chunk 1024), 3 runs | 2225 / 2254 / 2218 ms | 2111 / 2107 / 2112 ms |
| load + 2 tokens (warm page cache), 3 runs | 1.81-1.82 s | 2.58-2.64 s |

The load is slower: the device upload goes through a pinned bounce buffer.
A hipMemcpy straight from the mapping registers those pages with the driver,
which keeps them resident and faults them back after `madvise` - with the
7.24 GiB pinned on top, the kernel's reclaim failed (`kswapd0: page allocation
failure`) and decode steps stalled for up to 20 s.
