# #420 step 2: the decode step's launch and idle cost, re-measured on the current tree

2026-10-14, RX 9060 XT (gfx1200), the tree after #435/#436/#437 (+ the #439 record).
Prompt: 512 tokens (docs+PLAN repeated), `omph-run <omph> tok.txt out.f32 --last-logits --gemv
--generate N --gen-out g.txt [--draft-mtp 3] [--tokens 512]`, `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace`, and the
trace split at every argmax launch, so "forward" below is one pass of the model (a verification
or a plain greedy step), not the profiler's mixed average.

| forward | launches | busy ms | idle ms | span ms | idle % | us/launch |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| plain greedy, median of 33 | 553 | 40.19 | 1.98 | 42.17 | 4.7 | 3.58 |
| MTP k=3 verification, median of 20 | 702 | 42.47 | 2.48 | 44.95 | 5.5 | 3.53 |
| MTP draft (the 1-layer block), median of 114 | 20 | 0.23 | 0.13 | 0.36 | 36.1 | 6.50 |

The 65 generated tokens of the MTP run cost 20 x 44.95 + 114 x 0.36 = 940 ms, i.e. 14.5 ms per
token - the drafts are 4.4 % of it, the verifications 95.6 %. (3.25 tokens per verification: the
drafts are accepted well at this context.)

**The per-launch cost is unchanged** from #203 / #420 step 1 (3.5-3.6 us; ~6.5 us where a kernel
streams its weights). The 11 launches per layer are the two norms, swiglu, the attention prep /
merge, and the GEMV groups of the four projections.

## The decision, re-confirmed

The megakernel's *ceiling* is the idle column: 1.98-2.48 ms of a 42-45 ms forward (4.7-5.5 %),
and its realistic prize is less:

- the ideal memory-side fusion - one queued launch instead of a launch per segment - was measured
  in step 1 at 1.72 ms on a 37.3 ms chain of the step's own GEMV sizes (`launch-chain-420.txt`);
- the small kernels' fusion (the add + norm folded into the output GEMV's epilogue) was measured
  at +0.2 ms, a loss (`#103`) - and it is the same class of change: 3 of the 11 launches per layer;
- v0.6.0's own route to fewer launches, the sibling GEMV groups, is already in the engine
  (#214, #255: 1..16 tokens).

So ~1.7 ms of a 42 ms forward (4 %) for the largest and riskiest change in the project - device
side barriers, a work queue, per-type template instantiation, 64 grid syncs per step - against
the 36+ ms of GEMV time that is at 318 GB/s and that the engine's real levers (MTP, DFlash2,
n-gram drafts, the draft vocabulary, KV precision) have been cutting all along. **Not built;
closed as measured.**

Reopen if a future step becomes launch-bound - e.g. an int8 GEMV path halving the weight bytes, or
a GPU whose dispatch is cheaper than its DRAM.
