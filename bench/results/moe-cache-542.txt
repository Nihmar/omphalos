## Findings

1. The cache gives llama.cpp +9.5 % (edit-cc), +10 % (edit-py), +21 % (prose) plain decode
   over -ncmoe 16 at v0.7.0, and costs prefill -39 % / -14 % (large batches only read the
   cache; the misses run where the experts live, in host memory).
2. omphalos stays ahead on plain decode (1.6-1.8x) and on the best cell of edit-cc (2.1x) and
   prose. On edit-py llama.cpp's n-gram cell (142.7 t/s) beats omphalos' best (90.2): its text
   is a long copy and its n-gram drafts are long; omphalos' MoE verification reads an expert
   once per (token, expert) slot (#496), ~6.5 ms per verified row vs llama.cpp's ~2.8 ms
   (#551), and its n-gram drafts lose on edit-py (-13 % against MTP alone).
3. The first omphalos MTP + n-gram run of edit-cc stalled for ~8 s at the start (100 tokens at
   11.9 t/s, then 88-110 t/s; 71.8 t/s for the request) right after the llama.cpp cells
   released ~19 GB of pinned memory; a rerun of the cell (same text, same 1897 / 2221 drafts)
   ran at 96.1 and 97.7 t/s from the first token. The table has the rerun; the stalled run's
   rows were 71.8 / 77.7 / 89.9 t/s.
