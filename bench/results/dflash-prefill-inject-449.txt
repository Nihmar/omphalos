# #449: the DFlash2 prefill injection only for the rows the ring can hold

Engine main after #446 + this change, RX 9060 XT 16 GB, 2026-10-14.

## What was wasted

`dflash_inject` ran on every prefill chunk: the drafter's `fc` over the target
features, then the K/V projections of its 5 layers, for every prompt token. The
drafter's ring holds `dflash.attention.sliding_window` = 2048 rows, so for a
32,768-token prompt ~30,700 rows of that work were overwritten before the first
draft ever read them. The MTP block's window (#171) already fills only what it
keeps; this is the same idea one layer down.

`Runner::dflash_prompt(n)` (called with the prompt's length, next to
`mtp_prompt`) sets the first row worth projecting, `n - 2048`, and
`dflash_inject` clips its chunk to `[dflash_prompt, end)`: one branch plus the
row offsets on the `fc`, the norm and the five K/V blocks. The ring's *slots*
stay fully covered - 2048 consecutive positions mod 2048 are all of them - so no
tag is left stale from a skipped row.

## The prize, measured (`omph-run --dflash <drafter> --generate 2`, 5 runs each)

| prompt | full injection (`OMPH_DFLASH_FULL_INJECT=1`, pre-#449) | clipped (default) | change |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4096 | 3978.0 ms | 3954.5 ms | **-23.5 ms (-0.59 %)** |
| 32768 | 36368.6 ms | 36287.3 ms | **-81.3 ms (-0.22 %)** |

The 4096 cells spread 67 ms (3906-3973 against 3976-3979), so the small-prompt
gain is at the edge of the noise; the 32768 one is 81 ms against ±20 ms. Both are
in the 0.5-1 % the issue estimated, and the work removed is real by
construction.

## The witnesses: the ring at the end of the prompt is the same

- **Greedy tokens identical** with and without the clip, at 4096 and 32768 tokens
  (`cmp` of the two `--gen-out` files): the drafts the drafter produces after the
  prompt are unchanged, which is what the ring's content decides.
- **`check_conversations.py --spec dflash`** (all five cases: the two back-to-back
  turns, the compaction rewrite, the negative control, thinking off and on) passes
  with `--text` pointing at a local corpus: the saved conversations carry the
  drafter's ring and the resume path still answers exactly as the cold prefill,
  so the clipped chunks interact correctly with the checkpoints and the restored
  ring.

Adopted: the clip is the default, `OMPH_DFLASH_FULL_INJECT=1` restores the
pre-#449 behaviour for A/B.
