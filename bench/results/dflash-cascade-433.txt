# #433: the DFlash2 cascade (two trained blocks) — measured, not adopted

Engine main 0571d06 + the `feat/433-dflash-cascade` branch, RX 9060 XT 16 GB.
Prose prompt (283 tokens), 256 generated, `omph-run --last-logits --gemv
--generate 256 --dflash <drafter.omph>`, `OMPH_DFLASH_KEEP=0`, one block of 11
masks against `OMPH_DFLASH_BLOCKS=2` (two trained 7-mask blocks, the second
conditioned on the first through the drafter's ring). llama.cpp: v0.6.0 @
d81235049 (no llama.cpp run).

Correctness: the cascade is deterministic (two runs byte-identical) and its
tokens equal plain greedy (`cmp`). The per-position counters below are
`dflash_observe`'s (reached / kept, cumulative over the run).

The first block's seven positions accept about as well as the single block's
first seven (0.75 against 0.78 at position 7), so the *trained* shape is not the
problem. The second block's positions collapse (0.54 at its own position 1,
against the single block's 0.78 at position 8): the cascade loses on acceptance
*and* pays the second drafter pass, 5.30 -> 6.61 ms/token.

## Why the hypothesis failed

The cascade's second block is seeded with a *drafted* token and its context rows
are the first block's own K/V (published into the ring). The model's ring rows
are otherwise the *target's* features through `fc` (`dflash_inject`): the second
block therefore attends to a representation it never saw as context, and the
measured result says the long single block - out of training as it is - handles
those positions better. The block-diffusion mechanism (all rows inside one
non-causal block) conditions the far positions better than a ring-based
two-stage chain.

## Verdict

Not adopted. `OMPH_DFLASH_BLOCKS=2` stays as an off-by-default switch and the
diagnostics (`OMPH_DFLASH_DEBUG`'s per-position counters, `dfl_reach_` /
`dfl_kept_`) stay with it: they are what the 11-vs-15 medians of #414 were
missing, and they show the current design's real limit - the *chain's* cumulative
reach, not the block's training length.

The same counters also say what would move the needle: at position 8 the single
block still accepts 0.78, at 9 0.63, at 10 0.59, at 11 0.56 - the tail is worth
having, and the break-even is a marginal of ~0.11. The lever is therefore a
drafter whose *far* positions are better (a better draft model), not a different
way to chain the existing one.
