Measured ceilings of the RX 9060 XT (gfx1200, 16 WGPs), #203 phase 0, 2026-10-02, tree 0b32e24
Every figure is sustained (seconds of back-to-back launches, the clocks settled); a
cold single launch reads up to ~45 % low (LDS: 3.8 vs 7.0 TB/s), which is what
misled #213's first analysis.

DRAM read                318.3 GB/s        bench/bw_membench.hip (10 GiB, 10 passes, median)

                         = ~13.7 G 512-byte WMMA operands per second, above the f16 WMMA rate
WMMA, random register operands, 4 A x 4 B per wave, 5 s each (bench/gemm/wmma_rates.hip):
  f16  -> f32            88.7 TFLOPS
  bf16 -> f32            89.2 TFLOPS
  iu8  -> i32           168.6 TOPS          (1.9x f16: PLAN.md §10.5's "no int8 WMMA" was wrong)
  fp8  -> f32           166.4 TFLOPS
  iu4  -> i32 (16x16x32) 337.2 TOPS
  constant operands instead of random: f16 ~107 TFLOPS (less switching power)
