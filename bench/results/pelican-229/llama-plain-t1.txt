**How it works:**
- **Pedaling:** the crank arms rotate a full turn every 2 s, and the pelican's two legs are `d`-morphed paths whose feet trace the exact pedal-circle positions at 45° steps, so the feet stay planted on the pedals.
- **Rolling:** both wheels spin once per 2 s, and the scenery (tiles 320 px wide) scrolls 320 px in 2 s — the wheel circumference (~320 px) matches, so it looks like clean rolling with no slip.
- **Life:** the whole rig bounces gently, the wings flap, the beak dips, clouds drift in slow parallax, and dashed road lines plus speed lines convey motion.
