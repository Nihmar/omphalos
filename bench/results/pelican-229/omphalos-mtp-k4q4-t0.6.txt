**What's animated:**
- **Wheels** — spokes rotate continuously around each hub.
- **Crank & pedals** — rotate around the bottom bracket; each pedal counter-rotates so it stays level while pedaling.
- **Pelican legs** — the `d` attribute is keyframed so the feet track the pedal positions, with the far leg half a cycle out of phase.
- **Body/head/beak/wing** — the body bobs with the pedaling cadence, the head nods, the beak sways, and the folded wing flaps gently.
- **Environment** — the ground texture scrolls, clouds drift (seamless loop), the chain dashes move, and speed lines pulse behind the bike.
