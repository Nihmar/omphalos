Details worth noting:

- **Synchronized timing:** everything cycles on a 1.5 s period. Wheel radius is 50, so one revolution = 2π·50 ≈ 314 units — the road dashes scroll exactly 314 units and the chain's dash offset shifts 314 per loop, keeping ground speed consistent.
- **Feet track pedals:** each foot follows an `animateMotion` circle (radius 30, the crank throw) in phase with its pedal, while the leg line's endpoints animate to the same four quadrant points. The far leg/foot is drawn behind the frame in a darker shade for depth.
- **Level pedals:** pedals counter-rotate at the same rate as the crank, so they stay horizontal while orbiting — like a real bike.
- **Body bob:** the pelican rises twice per crank revolution (matching how a rider actually pumps up and down), and the wing sways slightly.
- **Parallax clouds** drift at different speeds, and fading speed lines add a sense of pace.
