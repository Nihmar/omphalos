**What's animated:**
- **Wheels & crank** — spokes and pedals rotate clockwise at 1 rev/sec, all synced.
- **Legs** — each leg's foot endpoint is animated around the pedal circle (8 sampled points per revolution), with the far leg drawn behind the frame and the near leg in front.
- **Pelican** — gentle vertical bob, subtle wing flap, and a wing-arm resting on the handlebar.
- **Background** — three parallax layers (clouds, bushes, road dashes) scroll left to sell the sense of speed, plus flickering speed lines.

Everything uses SMIL animations, so it runs natively in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge with no JavaScript.
