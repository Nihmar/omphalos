**How it works:**
- **Pedaling sync:** the crank rotates 360° in 1s, and both legs animate their path `d` through 9 keyframes matching the pedal circle (radius 35 around the bottom bracket). The far leg uses `begin="-0.5s"` so it's always opposite the near leg.
- **Wheels** spin clockwise at a speed roughly matching the ground scroll (road dashes at 360 px/s, trees slower for parallax).
- **Life in the rider:** the whole pelican bobs 3px, the far wing flaps, and the head/neck group nods slightly around the base of the neck.
- **Seamless loops:** scenery is duplicated over 1600px and translated by exactly one 800px period; clouds and speed lines fade or exit the frame before jumping.
