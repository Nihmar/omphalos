A few notes on how it works:

- **Wheels & crank** use `animateTransform` (rotate) — the wheels spin at 2× the crank for a believable gear ratio.
- **Legs** are two‑segment paths whose `d` attribute is morphed through 5 keyframes (top, front, bottom, back pedal positions), so the feet track the pedals. The far leg is offset by half a cycle (`begin="-0.5s"`) and drawn behind the frame.
- **Chain & ground** use animated `stroke-dashoffset` to scroll, and the **clouds** translate across for a parallax sense of motion.

You can tweak the `dur` values to speed up or slow down the whole ride.
