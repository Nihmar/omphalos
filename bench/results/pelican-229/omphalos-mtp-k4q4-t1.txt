Notes on how it works:

- **Two-segment pedaling legs**: each leg is a thigh group (rotating around the hip) with a nested shin group (rotating around the knee). The keyframe angles were computed from the pedal circle (crank radius 22 px) so the feet track the pedals over the full 360° stroke; the far leg runs 180° out of phase.
- **Consistent speed**: wheels, chain (dash offset), road dashes, and bushes all move at ~125 px/s to match the 1 rev/s crank, giving one consistent "cruise."
- **Pedals stay level** via counter-rotations nested inside the rotating crank group.
- Subtle life is added by the body bob, wing tuck, head/pouch sway, pulsing sun halo, and flickering speed lines.
