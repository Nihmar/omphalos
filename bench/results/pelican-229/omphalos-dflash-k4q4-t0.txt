**What's animated:**
- **Wheels** spin continuously (2s per revolution).
- **Crank and pedals** rotate once per second, in sync with the pedaling.
- **Legs** morph between four poses (top → front → bottom → back) so the feet track the pedals; the far leg is phase-shifted by half a cycle and drawn behind the bike.
- **Wing** flaps subtly, and the **head/neck** sways gently.
- **Whole rider** bobs up and down once per pedal stroke.
- **Road dashes and clouds** scroll leftward (at speeds matched to the wheel rotation) to create the illusion of forward motion.
