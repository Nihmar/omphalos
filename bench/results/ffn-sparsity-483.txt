# The FFN activation's block sparsity at the down projection's input (#483) — measured, not adopted

Engine main 2026-10-14 + an `OMPH_TEST_FFNDUMP=<file>` diagnostic (dump of `up_swiglu`'s `out16`,
the down projection's input; reverted). One 512-token prefill chunk, layer 0's FFN:
512 x 17408 values, max |x| 4.21, mean |x| 0.0024.

| 256 | < 0.1 | 92.6 % | 25.8 % | |

Energy concentration (256-blocks): 43 of 68 carry 90 %, 64.4 carry 99 %.

SwiGLU's output is never exactly zero: a bit-exact skip has nothing to skip. A tolerance-based
skip costs relative output noise ~sqrt(dropped energy share) on the down projection: 6.3 % of
the 256-blocks (0.25 % of the energy) for ~5 % noise, or 56 % (7.9 %) for ~28 % noise. The down
projections are 64 x 5120 x 17408 = 21 % of the per-token weight bytes, so the 6.3 % skip is
~1.3 % of a decode step — for a several-percent error on every layer's FFN output, on the path
that has been bit-exact by design (KV and the WY prefill are the only accepted approximations,
both with measured KL budgets).

Decision: measured, not adopted.
