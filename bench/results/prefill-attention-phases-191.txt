# #191: the prefill attention's phases, ablated on the current kernel

Engine main 1e151d0 + this branch, RX 9060 XT 16 GB (160 W cap), wikitext prompts
of **16384** and **74599** tokens (`omph-run <omph> ids /tmp/x.f32 --last-logits
--gemv`, `OMPH_TIMING`'s `prefill wall`, one run per cell; the baseline was run
twice at 74.6k: 123342 / 123748 ms, 0.3 % apart). llama.cpp: v0.6.0 @ d81235049
(no llama.cpp run here).

`OMPH_TEST_FA_ABL=N` (new, diagnostic, wrong results with valid timings) ablates
the prefill WMMA kernel's phases: **1** K staging, **2** V staging, **4** the
K q^T WMMAs, **8** the softmax (mask + max + exp2 + sum + P), **16** the V^T P
WMMAs, **32** the O rescale, **63** all. Each ablation removes only its own
work: the barriers, the LDS reads and the rest of the structure stay, so the
deltas are lower bounds and they overlap (the individual deltas sum to 35.7 s
against 33.4 s for the whole at 74.6k).

## Reading

- **The K q^T WMMAs are the critical path at long context**: 14 % of the whole
  prefill at 74.6k, against 2.2 % for the V^T P WMMAs - the same 32 WMMAs per
  (wave, 32-key tile). The difference is the dependency structure: the scores
  feed the softmax which feeds P, while the P V accumulates into O off the
  critical path.
- **The softmax is the other half at short context** (5.7 % at 16k, 5.0 % at
  74.6k) and it is at its ISA rates (#422: no packed exp, max3 worth ~3 % of
  it). It is a floor, not a lever.
- **The O rescale is free**: -0.0 % at 74.6k. Its 128 f32 multiplies per lane
  and tile are entirely hidden (which is why the lazy-rescale experiment of #191
  was a loss and stays dropped).
- **The staging is balanced against the consumers' second phase**: K 4.3 % + V
  3.5 % = 7.8 % against the softmax + P V's 7.2 %; the producers' 8 waves do
  nothing during phase 1 (V only) and everything during phase 2 (K).
- The residual -27.1 % - (16 % WMMAs + 5 % softmax + 7.8 % staging) leaves the
  barriers, the LDS traffic and the wave structure with a real share at long
  context, but removing any one phase also shortens the path the others wait on,
  so the decomposition cannot separate them further with this method.

## What the numbers point at (for the next restructuring round)

1. **Break the K q^T critical path**: 32 dependent WMMAs per wave per tile with
   only two independent chains (the two 16-key sub-tiles). Two key tiles in
   flight (64-key phases) would double the ILP but need **K double-buffered**
   (it is single-buffered today: `ks`, 8.4 KB; V already has two buffers) and
   ~8.4 KB more LDS (the workgroup is at 1 per WGP, VGPR-bound, so the LDS
   budget has room). The 64-key variant was rejected in #191's round 1 for LDS
   pressure *then*; the buffers have changed since.
2. **Rebalance the staging waves** across the two phases (e.g. half of K(t + 1)
   during phase 1) so the producer phase is not the longer of the two at 8
   waves.
3. Do not touch the softmax (at its rates, #422) or the rescale (free).
