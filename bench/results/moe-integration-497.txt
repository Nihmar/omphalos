## check_warm_server.py on the MoE: the prefill split moves a token

The first run (`--text` wikitext-2 test rebuilt as text) failed 4 checks, all
in the no-checkpoints variant (`--cache-ram 0`), all on the 2k prompt and its
second turn, in both orders; default, no-mtp, the canary and the cross-order
checks all passed. It is not state leaking between requests: two *fresh*
servers answer the 2k prompt differently,

    default         "... a Korean War engagement from September 1–15, 1950,"
    --cache-ram 0   "... a Korean War engagement from September 1-15, 1950,"

The checkpoints split the prompt's prefill at other positions, so its tail runs
through other kernels (the T <= 16 GEMV path and the short-chunk attention
instead of the GEMM and the flash attention); the rounding differs, and on the
MoE model the routing turns that into another expert somewhere and, here, an
en dash into a hyphen (the same effect as #516's KL). The dense model's
answers do not move with the split; a MoE model's can - llama.cpp's do with
its batch size too.

The check now judges a variant's answer that differs from the default fresh one
against a fresh server *of the same variant*: a leak fails, a split does not.
Rerun on the MoE: 115 checks, 0 failures; the four no-checkpoints answers that
differ from the default's (2k and 2k-turn2, both orders) are identical to a
fresh no-checkpoints server's; default, no-mtp and k8 match their references,
the two orders match each other in every variant, and the canary leaked
nowhere.
