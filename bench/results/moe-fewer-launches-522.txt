## Measured and not kept

- the router and the top-k in one kernel (the last block to finish selects):
  11.60 -> 11.56 ms, not worth an atomic counter and the fences
- an ablation that skipped the small kernels suggested ~11 us per launch: it
  left cur_ unwritten, the router saw garbage and chose only VRAM experts, so
  the PCIe reads vanished too; on this model an ablation that breaks the
  results also changes the routing
