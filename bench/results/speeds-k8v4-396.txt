# K8/V4 definitive speeds: omphalos vs llama.cpp, decode and prefill, with and without DFlash2 (#396)

Engine **main 9175738** (this branch adds results only). RX 9060 XT 16 GB, CachyOS, desktop
session active, card at its 182 W cap, no other GPU work, fresh processes for every cell.

llama.cpp: **the pin's RX 9060 XT overlay f19c20f9** (v0.6.0 base 6c7a87f7), the HIP reference
of `bench/llama.cpp.pin`. The upstream pin build d8123504 is *not* the K8/V4 reference: its
`GGML_CUDA_FA_QUANTS` has no `q8_0-q4_0`, so llama-server logs "converting K and V to f16
instead (slow)" and its tg128 at 32k is 14.20 against the overlay's 16.65 t/s (its numbers are
kept in the campaign B table for the record).

Model `Qwen3.8-27B-GSQ-RCO-IQ3_S-mtp.gguf` / `.omph`. KV: **K Q8 + V Q4 on every layer**
(omphalos `OMPH_KV_K4_LAYERS=none`; llama `-ctk q8_0 -ctv q4_0`). Greedy, context 32768.

## Reading

- **Prefill**: omphalos leads llama.cpp by 1.5-1.7x on a fresh prompt at every length, and the
  gap widens with the depth (llama's 512-chunk at 32k is 461 t/s against its 634 at depth 0).
- **Plain decode**: omphalos 22.5-24.4 t/s against llama's 16.7-20.6 (1.2-1.35x); the gap grows
  with the context (22.5 vs 16.7 at 32k), which is the KV/attention path.
- **DFlash2** (overlay): omphalos 43.3 t/s on prose against llama's 20.1 (2.2x, and llama's own
  DFlash2 is at parity with its plain decode there); with the n-gram on, 259 vs 206 on edit-cc,
  244 vs 160 on edit-py. MTP: 43.0 vs 23.3 on prose, 266.5 vs 200.0 on edit-cc, 229.1 vs 109.4
  on edit-py. Without any n-gram the drafter alone: dflash 134.9 vs 49.8 on edit-cc, 93.4 vs 38.4
  on edit-py.
- The n-gram is what makes the edit scenarios fast on both engines (llama's ngram-simple alone
  takes plain edit-cc from 20 to 175 t/s); omphalos' advantage there is the drafter's chain
  length on top of it.
- Caveats: one run per cell (the #262 convention); the two engines' outputs diverge at the same
  config (KV numerics), so the token counts and the content after the divergence differ; the
  scenarios are short prompts (34-4073 tokens, the generation growing the context).
